# Khushboo Landing Page Design System

## Overview

Khushboo is positioned as an executive luxury fragrance brand for
professional men in Karachi. The experience is designed to transform a
perfume purchase into an identity purchase: confidence, presence,
sophistication, and memorability.

The system combines: - Luxury black-and-gold visual language - Cinematic
fragrance storytelling - Premium product photography -
Conversion-focused ecommerce hierarchy - Strong emotional positioning

Core message:

> "Leave a Lasting Impression Before You Say a Word."

## Brand Direction

### Audience

Professional men who want a signature fragrance for: - Office
environments - Business meetings - Social occasions - Personal
confidence

### Value Proposition

A premium 100ml men's fragrance offering: - Fresh citrus opening - Warm
smoky masculine depth - Long-lasting presence - Affordable luxury
pricing

## Color System

### Midnight Black

Primary background and luxury foundation.

### Champagne Gold

Used for: - CTA buttons - Premium highlights - Pricing emphasis - Brand
details

### Amber Brown

Used in photography to communicate: - Warmth - Tobacco notes - Mystery

### Cream White

Used sparingly for readable luxury contrast.

## Typography

Typography should communicate premium editorial luxury.

Primary pairing: - Playfair Display for emotional luxury headlines -
Inter for ecommerce clarity and conversion elements

Avoid: - Playful fonts - Trendy display styles - Excessive text
decoration

## Layout Principles

### Hero Section

Desktop: - Two-column composition - Product image positioned right -
Copy positioned left - Large negative space

Content hierarchy: 1. Brand statement 2. Emotional headline 3. Fragrance
description 4. CTA 5. Trust indicators

### Product Story Sections

Use large cinematic images: - Fresh opening notes - Smoky masculine base
notes - Professional lifestyle moments

Maintain generous spacing between sections.

## Components

### Primary CTA

Purpose: Drive purchase action.

Style: - Gold background - Dark text - Premium rectangular shape -
Strong contrast

Examples: - Buy Khushboo Now - Order Your Signature Scent

### Trust Cards

Use: - Customer reviews - Star ratings - COD availability - Delivery
guarantee

### Offer Banner

Promotion: - Rs 2000 launch price - 25% discount - Free delivery until
15 September - Cash on Delivery

## Fragrance Story

### Top Notes

-   Pineapple
-   Lemon
-   Bergamot
-   Pear
-   Plum

Emotion: Fresh confidence.

### Heart Notes

-   Cedarwood
-   Tobacco
-   Patchouli

Emotion: Professional sophistication.

### Base Notes

-   Sandalwood
-   Musk
-   Smoky accords

Emotion: Memorable presence.

## Photography Direction

Style: - Dark cinematic lighting - Luxury fragrance campaigns - Black
marble surfaces - Golden reflections - Soft smoke - Premium masculine
environments

Avoid: - Bright ecommerce backgrounds - Cheap product styling - Generic
perfume imagery

## Conversion Principles

Every section should answer:

1.  Why should I care?
2.  Why should I trust this?
3.  Why should I buy now?

Conversion triggers: - Social proof - Limited launch offer - COD
reassurance - Premium positioning - Clear repeated CTA

## Responsive Behavior

Desktop: - Full cinematic hero - Wide product storytelling sections

Mobile: - Product-first hero - Stacked content sections - Large
touch-friendly CTA buttons - Simplified navigation

## Do's

-   Maintain black/gold luxury aesthetic
-   Prioritize product photography
-   Use emotional storytelling
-   Keep CTA visible throughout the page
-   Highlight customer reviews

## Don'ts

-   Don't use bright retail colors
-   Don't overcrowd sections
-   Don't compete on low price
-   Don't use generic perfume messaging

## Final Design Principle

Khushboo is not selling a bottle.

Khushboo is selling the feeling of being remembered.
